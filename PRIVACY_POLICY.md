## 2. Information We Collect

1. **Account & Identity Data:** Full name, email address, Firebase authentication UID, profile preferences, preferred interface language, and display currency.
2. **Subscription & Billing Data:** Subscription tier, billing cycle (monthly/annual), payment status, and transaction history. *(Note: Full credit card numbers and CVVs are processed directly and securely by Stripe, Apple, or Google, and are never stored on MarketMind servers).*
3. **Usage & Analytical Data:** Log data, feature usage frequency, API call volumes, deep research request counters, saved reports, watchlist configurations, and custom alert rules.
4. **Device & Connection Data:** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, and session timestamps for security, fraud prevention, and audit compliance.
5. **Connected Brokerage Tokens (Optional):** If you connect a brokerage (e.g. Alpaca, Interactive Brokers), encrypted OAuth tokens and read-only portfolio balances are stored securely to calculate portfolio beta and risk metrics. We never execute unauthorized live trades.

## 6. Data Retention & Security Measures

1. **Security Standards:** All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3/HTTPS. Sensitive tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
2. **Retention Period:** We retain account and research data for the duration of your active account. If you cancel your subscription, your saved watchlists and research dossiers remain accessible on the Free Starter plan unless you request complete account deletion.
