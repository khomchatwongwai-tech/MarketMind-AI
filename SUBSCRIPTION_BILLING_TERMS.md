## 2. 15-Day Free Trial Terms & Policy

1. **One Trial Per User:** The 15-day free trial is available exclusively to new accounts. Once a trial has been initiated or expired, the user cannot initiate a secondary trial on that account.
2. **No Automatic Hostage:** Upon expiration of the 15-day trial period, the account automatically downgrades to the Free Starter plan unless you explicitly initiate an active paid subscription. Your saved watchlists, custom alerts, and historical research reports are never deleted.
3. **Milestone Notifications:** Users receive clear in-app notices at Day 1 (Welcome), Day 10 (5 days remaining), Day 14 (Ends tomorrow), and upon expiration.

## 3. Recurring Billing & Automatic Renewal

1. **Billing Cycle:** Subscriptions are billed in advance on a recurring monthly or annual basis depending on your selection.
2. **Automatic Renewal:** Your subscription will automatically renew at the conclusion of each billing period at the then-current standard rate, unless you cancel prior to the renewal date.
3. **Pricing Transparency:** Launch pricing is locked in USD. We will provide at least 30 days prior written notice via email before implementing any price adjustments for existing active subscriptions.

## 4. Cancellation & Access Retention Policy

1. **Self-Service Cancellation:** You may cancel your subscription at any time with zero friction through:
   - **Web:** Account Settings → Subscription & Entitlements → "Manage Subscription" (Stripe Customer Portal) or "Cancel Plan".
   - **Apple iOS / iPadOS:** Device Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → MarketMind AI.
   - **Google Play / Android:** Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions → MarketMind AI.
2. **Period-End Access:** Following cancellation, you maintain full access to all tier entitlements until the end of your prepaid billing period (`currentPeriodEnd`). No further automatic charges will occur.
3. **Data Preservation:** Canceling a paid plan never wipes your research or user profile. Your account safely reverts to the Free Starter tier.
